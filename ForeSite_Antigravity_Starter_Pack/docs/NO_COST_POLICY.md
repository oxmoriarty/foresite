# No-cost policy

No billing activation, Blaze upgrade, paid API, paid model, paid hosting, paid dependency, or paid infrastructure. Do not treat optional Google Account Quota as a permanent/free production entitlement: the contest brief says it lasts four days, is not guaranteed, and usage beyond the quota/window may incur fees.

Before adding a Firebase product, verify current official pricing/plan requirements, free quotas, throttling, eligibility, model access, and data terms. Test the actual path without billing. If a required capability cannot be tested under the no-billing constraint, report the blocker; do not bypass it. Never promise unlimited free use. Use synthetic data during development.
